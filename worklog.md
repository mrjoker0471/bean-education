- Multi-page platform rendered as an SPA at the single visible route `/` (src/app/page.tsx) with hash deep-links: `#/`, `#/catalog`, `#/exam/<slug>`, `#/topic/<id>`, `#/test/<attemptId>`, `#/result/<attemptId>`, `#/bean`, `#/progress`. No other page routes.
